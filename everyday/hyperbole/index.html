hyperbole

hy-PER-buh-lee

IPA unavailable

Listen

Dictionary clips play when available; otherwise browser speech after you click. Native appears only when a non-US/UK source clip exists.

Phonetic spelling
hy-PER-buh-lee
Syllables
4
Stress
Primary stress early — prefer “hy-PER-buh-lee”

Definition

n

(uncountable, rhetoric, literature) Deliberate or unintentional overstatement, particularly extreme overstatement.

n

(countable) An instance or example of such overstatement.

n

(countable, geometry, obsolete) A hyperbola.

Common mispronunciation

Avoid: HY-per-bowl.

Prefer: hy-PER-buh-lee

Ends like bolee, not bowl.

Related words

Practice

  1. Play US, then UK — note the first differing vowel.
  2. Primary stress early — prefer “hy-PER-buh-lee”. Shadow Slow once.
  3. Say “hyperbole” three times from memory, then replay normal speed.

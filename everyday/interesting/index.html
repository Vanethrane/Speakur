This is a dedicated page at /everyday/interesting/. Audio uses free dictionary clips when available, otherwise browser speech after you click Play.

Meaning

verb

To engage the attention of; to awaken interest in; to excite emotion or passion in, in behalf of a person or thing.

“Action films don't really interest me.”

verb

To be concerned with or engaged in; to affect; to concern; to excite.

verb

To cause or permit to share.

adjective

Of concern; affecting, important.

adjective

Arousing or holding the attention or interest of someone.

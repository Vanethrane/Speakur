espresso

ess-PRESS-oh

/ɛˈspɹɛsəʊ/

Listen

Dictionary clips play when available; otherwise browser speech after you click. Native appears only when a non-US/UK source clip exists.

IPA
/ɛˈspɹɛsəʊ/ · /ɛˈspɹɛsoʊ/
Phonetic spelling
ess-PRESS-oh
Syllables
3
Stress
Primary stress on 2nd of 3 syllables

Definition

noun

A concentrated coffee beverage brewed by forcing hot water under high pressure through finely ground coffee.

noun

A drink that includes espresso as an ingredient.

Common mispronunciation

Avoid: ex-PRESS-oh.

Prefer: ess-PRESS-oh

No x sound at the start.
